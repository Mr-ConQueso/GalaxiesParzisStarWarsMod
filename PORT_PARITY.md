Use `develop` `312074c01` for behavior shared with the current bundle. The release tag `0.0.109+1.20.1` (`bc8f64c5c`) establishes released legacy gameplay. The legacy additions also use the Minecraft 1.20.2 branch `master` at `05b81f778` for playable equipment, vehicles, species, creatures, utilities, and construction. A slice reaches parity after its relevant single-player, dedicated-server, and visual checks pass.
